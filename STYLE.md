## Document grammar (the 3-slide commentary pattern; adapt per output type)
1. **Fund + manager identity**: fund name as title, partner firm's investment
   approach in 3-4 bullet phrases, PM names + headshots, market context narrative.
2. **The update**: "MONTHLY PORTFOLIO UPDATE" banner; three labelled commentary
   blocks — Performance Commentary / Attribution Commentary / Fund Positioning.
   Numbers always benchmark-relative with the benchmark named.
3. **Contact + disclaimer**: standard CISCA disclaimer block (see below), contact
   details, sources.

## Voice
- Plain-English institutional. Explanatory metaphors welcome ("local fixed income
  did the heavy lifting") but at most one per section.
- Every performance number is relative: "0.9% over one month versus 0.6% for the
  benchmark". Name the benchmark in full on first use.
- Explain WHY, not just what: attribution over description.
- No hype words (thrilled, excited, incredible). No exclamation marks. Confident,
  measured, client-respectful.
- Past performance caveats and "medium to long-term investments" framing are
  mandatory in client-facing materials.
- Numbers: one decimal place for percentages; "R11.3 billion" format for AUM.

## Standard disclaimer (client-facing materials — copy verbatim)
"Nedgroup Collective Investments (RF) Proprietary Limited administers the Nedgroup
Investments unit trust portfolios and is authorised to do so as a manager in terms of
the Collective Investment Schemes Control Act. Collective Investment Schemes (unit
trusts) are generally medium to long-term investments. The value of participatory
interests (units) or the investment may go down as well as up and past performance is
not necessarily a guide to future performance. Nedgroup Investments does not guarantee
the performance of your investment and the investor will carry the investment and
market risk, which includes the possibility of losing capital."

## Per-channel notes
- **PPTX/PDF packs**: 16:9, white background, deep-green section banners, bright
  green used sparingly for data highlights and dividers. Logo top-left or top-right,
  partner logo opposite. Footer: "Source: Nedgroup Investments, <as-at date>".
- **LinkedIn**: study ingestion/linkedin/*.md for cadence — hook line, 3-5 short
  paragraphs, occasional emoji at line starts (🎧👉📌, sparingly), hashtags at end
  (#NedgroupInvestments #Nedbank + 2-3 topical). Always a CTA link line.
- **Client email**: subject = fund + period; 150-250 words; one key number bolded;
  disclaimer footer link, not full block.
- **WhatsApp summary**: <=600 chars, no markdown headers, 2-3 bullets + one line
  "reply for the full pack".
