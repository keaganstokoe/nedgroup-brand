## Slide 2 — Fund and manager
Section label: FUND AND MANAGER          Period (top-right): Q2 2026
Headline (Georgia): Local bonds rebounded on the Fitch upgrade
Left column — INVESTMENT APPROACH: Diversified across local and offshore fixed income asset classes. Capital moved away from expensive asset classes toward undervalued ones. Priority on high credit quality: senior bank debt and high-grade corporates. PORTFOLIO MANAGERS: Lara Dalmeyer and Philip Liebenberg, Abax Investments.
Right column — Market context: The second quarter of 2026 was volatile, marked by a geopolitical energy shock stemming from the conflict involving Iran and a subsequently hawkish pivot from the US Federal Reserve. Despite this, South African assets diverged sharply from the global mood: the JSE/FTSE All Bond Index (ALBI) surged 7.9% for the quarter, supported by Fitch's upgrade of South Africa's sovereign credit rating to BB, the country's first upgrade in nearly 21 years, and sustained foreign inflows into local bonds of R139 billion over the preceding 18 months.

Left: small "01" in grey, then block heading (Georgia, green): Performance Commentary
Right: The Nedgroup Investments Flexible Income Fund returned 3.0% over three months versus 1.8% for the 110% STeFI Call Deposit benchmark, and 9.8% over twelve months versus 7.0% for the benchmark. The three-month result marks a strong rebound from a more muted first quarter, as South African fixed income markets recovered sharply following the earlier bout of risk-off volatility. The twelve-month result, close to three percentage points ahead of benchmark, shows that the Fund's return was not simply a function of elevated cash rates, but reflected value added through active allocation across South African fixed income.

## Slide 4 — Attribution Commentary
Same layout as slide 3 with "02" and heading Attribution Commentary.
Right: South African fixed income did the heavy lifting. The ALBI's 7.9% surge over the quarter, underpinned by Fitch's upgrade of South Africa's sovereign credit rating and continued foreign bond inflows, drove strong contributions from the Fund's holdings in local nominal bonds. Inflation-linked bonds also performed well, gaining 6.5% over the quarter and adding to returns. Offshore exposure contributed comparatively little to the quarter's return, consistent with the Fund's modest allocation to offshore bonds and money market instruments.

## Slide 5 — Fund Positioning
Same layout as slide 3 with "03" and heading Fund Positioning.
Right: At quarter-end the Fund held a conservative domestic duration of 1.0 years in nominal bonds and 0.2 years in inflation-linked bonds, having opportunistically added duration into the weakness experienced during the first quarter of 2026. Credit quality remained high, with fixed income exposure concentrated in senior bank debt. Offshore bonds and money market instruments accounted for 5.3% of the portfolio and domestic property for 2.3%, keeping the Fund anchored in South African fixed income and its carry, credit spread and interest-rate opportunities.
